## CONCEPT THEMES (Non-AI Focused)

### 1. The Ordinary Made Strange
**Core idea:** Finding depth in everyday objects and moments
- **"The Weight of a Key"** - What we carry, physically and metaphorically
- **"What Shadows Know"** - How shadows reveal what light conceals
- **"The Corner of Your Eye"** - What we see when we're not quite looking

### 2. Time and Change
**Core idea:** How we experience duration and transformation
- **"Waiting"** - What happens in the pause between moments
- **"The Last Time You Did Something"** - Without knowing it was the last
- **"How Things Wear"** - The story objects tell through use

### 3. Space and Place
**Core idea:** How environments shape experience
- **"The Doorway Effect"** - Why we forget when we enter a room
- **"Empty Rooms"** - What spaces feel like without people
- **"The View From Here"** - How perspective literally changes everything

### 4. Small Gestures
**Core idea:** The meaning in micro-actions
- **"The Pause Before Speaking"** - What happens in that breath
- **"Closing a Book"** - The moment between finishing and moving on
- **"Looking Up"** - What we find when we change our angle

### 5. Natural Patterns
**Core idea:** Emergence and beauty in non-AI contexts
- **"Why Rivers Curve"** - The path of least resistance
- **"The Edge of the Forest"** - Where two worlds meet
- **"Cracks"** - How breaking reveals structure

## QUALITY CRITERIA (Self-Assessment)

Before developing any concept:
1. Can this be experienced, not just explained?
2. Does it work as a visual piece, not just narration?
3. Will someone see the world differently after watching?
4. Is there a surprise, reveal, or shift in perspective?
5. Does it connect to universal human experience?
